Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 on digital operational resilience for the financial sector
Article 5 — Governance and organisation
1.

The management body of the financial entity defines, approves, oversees and is responsible for the implementation of all arrangements related to the ICT risk management framework.

2.

The management body bears the ultimate responsibility for managing ICT risk and sets the relevant policies, roles and responsibilities.

3.

Financial entities other than microenterprises establish a role to monitor relevant ICT third-party arrangements or designate senior management responsibility.

4.

Members of the management body maintain sufficient knowledge and skills to understand and assess ICT risk.
